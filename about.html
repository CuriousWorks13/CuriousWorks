About Us

Started by students who never stopped asking "why."

Curious Works began with a simple frustration: school teaches you what you need to know, rarely what you'd love to know. Here's our story, our mission, and where we're headed.

Our Story

Why Curious Works was created

Curious Works started as a weekend project between a handful of high schoolers who kept noticing the same thing in younger siblings, cousins, and neighbors: kids are endlessly curious until the school year narrows what "counts" as worth learning.

We asked a simple question: what if the topics that made us fall in love with learning were taught earlier, by people close enough in age to make it feel exciting rather than intimidating? Curious Works is our answer.

◎

Founded by high schoolers

Every course, lesson plan, and program decision at Curious Works passes through students first, because we're the ones building it.

Our Mission

Make curiosity a habit, not an accident.

We exist to expose elementary and middle school students to ideas beyond the standard curriculum, and to make that exposure feel like discovery, not homework.

1

Expose

Introduce students to fields and ideas they may never otherwise encounter before high school, or at all.

2

Engage

Teach with energy and clarity, using instructors who remember exactly what makes a lesson click or fall flat.

3

Empower

Leave every student with the confidence that they can understand something that once looked out of reach.

★

Not a tutoring service

We don't reteach the school curriculum. We go around it, into subjects students wouldn't otherwise see.

★

Peer-built curriculum

Every lesson is designed by high schoolers who recently learned how to make complex ideas click.

★

Breadth over drilling

★

Built to scale

What Makes Us Different

We're closer to the student than any traditional program.

Most enrichment programs are built by adults, for kids, based on what adults remember (or assume) kids want. Curious Works is built by people who were elementary and middle schoolers recently enough to remember exactly what made a subject exciting, or completely lose them.

That closeness shapes everything: how we write lessons, how we run sessions, and how we talk about hard topics in ways that don't feel like a textbook.

Who We Teach

Elementary and middle school students, wherever they are.

Our courses are grouped by grade band so every session is pitched at the right level of depth and pace.

Grades 3–5

Hands-on, visual introductions to big ideas, built around curiosity, stories, and simple experiments.

Grades 6–8

Deeper dives with more independent thinking, discussion, and project-based work.

Families & Schools

We work with parents directly and are building partnerships with schools and community organizations.

Our vision for the future

We're building toward a world where every student, regardless of school, zip code, or budget, has access to instructors who make learning about anything feel possible. This is the beginning, not the whole story.
