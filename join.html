Join Us

Help push curiosity a little further.

Curious Works runs entirely on people who care about this mission: instructors, volunteers, and partners. Here's how to get involved.

◆

Become an Instructor

Design and teach a course in a subject you love. We provide training and curriculum support.

  • High school students (grades 9–12)
  • No prior teaching experience required
  • Flexible, part-time commitment
◆

Volunteer

Support live sessions, help with logistics, or assist instructors during class time.

  • Great for a first-time volunteer
  • Flexible scheduling
  • Remote and in-person options
◆

Help with Curriculum

Research topics, build lesson plans, and design activities used across multiple courses.

  • Good fit for detail-oriented planners
  • Work at your own pace
  • Direct input on what gets taught
◆

Help with Outreach

Spread the word to families, schools, and communities who could benefit from Curious Works.

  • Social media and content
  • School and community partnerships
  • Event coordination
◆

Become a Partner

Schools, nonprofits, and community organizations can bring Curious Works programming to their students.

  • Co-hosted sessions or workshops
  • Custom scheduling
  • Long-term collaboration
◆

Support Curious Works

Help fund materials, platforms, and outreach that keep courses free or low-cost for families.

  • One-time or recurring support
  • Sponsor a course or classroom
  • Contact us for details
